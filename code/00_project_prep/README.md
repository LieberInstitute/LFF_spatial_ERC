
## Polygenic Risk Scores 

GWAS stats are results of European ancestry samples described in these papers:
    
1. Genome-wide meta-analysis identifies new loci and functional pathways influencing Alzheimer’s disease risk,  Nature Genetics, 2019 Mar;51(3):404-413.

2. A genome-wide association study with 1,126,563 individuals identifies new risk loci for Alzheimer’s disease.  Nature  Genet. 2021 Sep;53(9):1276-1282. 

3. New insights into the genetic etiology of Alzheimer’s disease and related dementias, Nature Genetics volume 54, pages412–436 (2022).
